abstract: >-
  Deep learning models can perform very well on clean images but may become less reliable when the input images are affected by noise, blur, weather effects, or other types of corruption. In this study, we examine how the behavior of a ResNet-50 image classification model changes as image corruption becomes more severe. Evaluating on CIFAR-10-C across 15 corruption types and five severity levels, we measure model confidence, Jensen–Shannon divergence, prediction flip rate, and Grad-CAM explanation instability. Results show behavioral instability provides the strongest early warning of degradation, crossing the warning threshold earlier for 9 of 15 corruption types with an average lead of 2 severity levels before accuracy drops below 80%.
