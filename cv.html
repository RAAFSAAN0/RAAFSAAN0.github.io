Latent-Space Anomaly Detection via Explainable AI for Enhancing GAN Robustness

  • Developed an explainable anomaly detection framework for identifying adversarial perturbations in GAN latent spaces.
  • Proposed a dual-autoencoder approach combining a deterministic LatentExplainer and a variational LatentVAE to model deviations from the learned latent manifold.
  • Investigated the proposed framework across WGAN-GP and StyleGAN2 architectures using MNIST, CIFAR-10, and CelebA datasets.
  • Evaluated the framework under adversarial attacks including FGSM, PGD, and CW.
  • Investigated latent-space representations and explainability as mechanisms for detecting adversarial perturbations in generative models.

Research Projects
Monitoring Model Degradation Under Progressive Image Corruption
2026

PyTorch | CIFAR-10-C | Explainable AI | Grad-CAM

  • Evaluated an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-50 on CIFAR-10-C across 15 corruption types and five severity levels to characterize model degradation under progressive image corruption.
  • Analyzed confidence change, Jensen-Shannon divergence, and prediction flip rate as behavioral signals for detecting emerging performance degradation.
  • Demonstrated that behavioral instability provided early warning for 9 of 15 corruption types, with a mean lead of 2 severity levels before accuracy fell below 80%.
