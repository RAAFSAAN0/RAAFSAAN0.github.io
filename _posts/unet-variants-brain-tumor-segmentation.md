## Overview

A comparative study of **3D U-Net, Residual 3D U-Net, and U-Net 3+** for brain tumor segmentation using the **BraTS20** dataset. The models were evaluated using **Dice, Jaccard, and mIoU** metrics, with U-Net 3+ achieving the strongest overall segmentation performance.

## Models Evaluated

* **3D U-Net**
* **Residual 3D U-Net**
* **U-Net 3+**

## Evaluation

The models were evaluated using:

* **Dice Score**
* **Jaccard Index**
* **mIoU**

## Results

U-Net 3+ achieved the strongest segmentation performance among the evaluated architectures, demonstrating the benefits of improved feature aggregation and multi-scale information for brain tumor segmentation.
